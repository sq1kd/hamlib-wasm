Connect a radio over USB, pick its model and press **Connect your radio**. It
needs desktop Chrome, Edge or Firefox 151+. Until a radio is connected, the
panel plays sample readings.

<LiveDemo client:only="react" />

:::caution[It writes to your radio]
Unlike the read-only panel on the home page, this one sets frequency, mode
and passband - and **TX on** keys the transmitter. Mind your antenna.
:::

**Auto-detect** tries the rates the model supports, highest first, and
remembers the one that answered, so the next connect tries it first. Picking
the rate set in the radio's menu skips the search.

Tick **Show library calls** to see what the panel does under the hood. The log
also lets you choose, before connecting, which of two ways drives the **same**
rig and the same Hamlib module:

- **Rig** - `rig.setMode(mode)`, `rig.setPassband('narrow')` and two
  `useRigState` polls: frequency, mode and PTT once a second, and the S-meter
  every 250 ms;
- **Raw** - `rig.raw`, the underlying `RawRig`: `getFrequency()`, `getMode()` and
  `getPtt()` polled by hand, and `setMode(mode, width)` with an explicit width
  from `passbandNormal()`.

The demo loads with this page only: it fetches the Hamlib WebAssembly module
and its loader, a few megabytes, which no other page of this site does. If your
browser asks to save data, it waits for a click instead.
